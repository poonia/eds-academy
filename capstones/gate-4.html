Gate 4: Enterprise reference architecture

Multi-brand platform design, governance model, block catalog, migration plan and operating runbook.

The brief

Level 4 · Expert · About 30 to 40 hours. Content as of 9 Nov 2026.

Acme Financial has three brands, a legacy platform, regulated content and several delivery teams. Leadership wants a target design and a credible way to get there. This is a design capstone: no code is needed.

Acme Financial is fictional. Use only sites and assets you have permission to use.

What to build

  • A multi-brand platform design: repositories, sites, profiles, content sources, CDN
  • A governance model, including RACI
  • A block catalog with owners and standards
  • A migration plan in stages, with exit criteria
  • An operating runbook: monitoring, incidents, rollback and support tiers
  • Decision records for the major choices

Suggested milestones

State goals, constraints and non-goals. Confirm compliance needs.

Compare at least two architectures, and record your decision.

Write the RACI, catalog rules and authoring controls.

Stage the migration, and set go or no-go criteria.

Write the runbook and support model. Run a mock incident.

Present to a reviewer, and answer challenges.
