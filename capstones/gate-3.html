Gate 3: Migrated section

A real or sample site section moved to EDS with redirects, analytics in the delayed phase, CSP and a go-live checklist.

The brief

Level 3 · Advanced · About 30 to 45 hours. Content as of 9 Nov 2026.

Acme Financial is moving its Savings section from a legacy platform. It must keep its search value, and meet security requirements. Use a real site you have permission to copy, or a sample site.

Acme Financial is fictional. Use only sites and assets you have permission to use.

What to build

  • A section of at least 20 pages, imported with repeatable rules
  • A component-to-block mapping
  • Validated redirects for changed URLs
  • Analytics that load late, and that respect consent
  • A nonce-based Content Security Policy
  • A completed go-live checklist, and a rehearsal on a staging host, if you have a CDN

Suggested milestones

List URLs, traffic and page types. Decide what to keep.

Map components to default content and blocks.

Write rules, test samples with an author, then bulk import.

Add the CSP, consent-aware analytics and roles.

Compare parity. Test redirects.

Walk through the go-live checklist, including rollback.
