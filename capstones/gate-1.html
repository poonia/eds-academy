Gate 1: Personal microsite

An aem.live site with nav, footer, 5 pages and a custom block scoring 100 on mobile Lighthouse.

The brief

Level 1 · Beginner · About 10 to 15 hours. Content as of 9 Nov 2026.

Acme Financial is sponsoring a small community financial literacy program. You have been asked to build its first site on Edge Delivery Services, as your own project.

Acme Financial is fictional. Use only sites and assets you have permission to use.

What to build

  • A site on aem.live, created from the boilerplate
  • A navigation and a footer, authored as content
  • At least five pages, using sections, default content, blocks and metadata
  • One custom block that you wrote, with a documented content model
  • A mobile Lighthouse score of 100 on the published page with your block

Suggested milestones

Sketch five pages. Mark what is default content, and what needs a block.

Create the repository, install Code Sync, and connect a content source.

Write the pages, nav and footer. Preview them.

Write your block on a branch, with lint passing.

Fix Lighthouse results on your branch URL, and test by keyboard.

Merge through a pull request, publish, and collect your evidence.
