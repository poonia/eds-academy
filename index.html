A self-paced course on Adobe Edge Delivery Services. Three lines share one network of 45 modules and 253 topics, and every line stops at the same four capstone gates.

  • Author: write and publish pages
  • Developer: build blocks and integrations
  • Implementation lead: launch, run and govern

Choose your line

Each line is an order through the modules for one role. You can change lines at any interchange, and your progress comes with you.

The network

Each line runs through four levels, from beginner to expert. Black-ringed stations are interchanges shared by more than one line, and a gate ends each level.

What happens at each station

Every module follows the same four stops, so you always know what comes next.

  1. ReadA short concept with a diagram and a real example.
  2. TryA quick check, a simulator or a debug case, right in the page.
  3. BuildA hands-on lab or code lab against real EDS conventions.
  4. KeepA cheat sheet and flashcards for the next time you need it.

About 109.5 hours for all 45 modules, plus capstones and exams. You don't need EDS experience to start. No sign-up, and progress stays in this browser. An independent resource, not official Adobe documentation. Last reviewed against Adobe's docs on 9 November 2026.
