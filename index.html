Is this course for you?

A practical course for people who create, build or run sites on Edge Delivery Services. You don't need EDS experience to start.

Who it's for

Content authors and publishers, front-end developers, and the leads and architects who launch and run sites. Follow one track or all three.

What you'll be able to do

Publish well-structured pages, build fast and accessible blocks, then configure, launch, monitor and govern a site.

How you'll learn

Short lessons with a diagram, an example and a quick check. Each module has an interactive simulator, a hands-on lab and a cheat sheet.
